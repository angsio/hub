A scheduling board for tasks, events and reminders, with an assistant that can
read your schedule and add to it when you ask.

## What it does

Everything sits under a theme you create, like Work or Personal. You can look at
it as a board of columns or as a weekly timetable, and the same items appear in
both. Tasks can carry a deadline, events run from one time to another, and
reminders sit at a single moment. It works on a phone as well as a desktop, with
the layout thinning out rather than rearranging itself.

The assistant lives beside the timetable. Ask what is due this week and it reads
your board before answering. Ask it to book something straight after your Monday
meeting and it looks that meeting up first, works out when the meeting ends,
then shows you exactly what it plans to create and waits. Nothing reaches the
database until you press confirm, and if you decline it says so rather than
pretending the job is done.

## The part I am most pleased with

The assistant does not start with a list of things it can do. It starts with one
ability: to go looking for one. When your message arrives it decides whether it
needs to touch your schedule at all, and if it does, it describes the capability
it wants in its own words, gets that tool back, and uses it. If the next step
needs something different, it goes looking again.

Two things fall out of that. Saying hello costs nothing, because it never
reaches for a tool it does not need. And it cannot invent what is on your
schedule, because the only route to knowing is to go and read it.

## One account for everything

Signing in once covers the whole domain. The same account works here and on
anything I add later, each account sees only its own board, and there is one
login page instead of a password per app. The session lives in a cookie scoped
to the parent domain, so every subdomain recognises you without anything being
passed between them.

## Built with

React and Tailwind on the front, Express and MongoDB behind it. All of it is
self hosted on a Raspberry Pi at home and reaches the internet through a
Cloudflare tunnel, so no port on my router is open.

## What I would change

With only three tools to choose from, the search that picks them matches
everything, so that part is built for a scale it has not reached yet. It earns
its keep at thirty tools, not at three. There is also no password reset, and a
board left open in two tabs will not update the second one until it is reloaded.
